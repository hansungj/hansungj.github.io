Sungjun Han

Research at Trillion Labs

I work on language models, from pretraining and data to post-training and evaluation. At Trillion Labs I am leading the development of Korean open-source foundation models — the Tri series and Gravity — working across the full stack, from data collection to pretraining and post-training.

A question I keep coming back to is how language models generalize beyond what they have seen, and how we can understand that generalization before it becomes apparent at scale. My earlier work focused on compositional generalization; more recently, rBridge looks at how smaller models can predict signals that only emerge later in pretraining.

I am interested in how these ideas can inform the training of more capable models and agents: how models can combine capabilities across multiple steps, and what should be learned in the model versus provided by the surrounding system. I am also interested in world models, and how they can help us train agents that learn and plan through interaction. More broadly, I like building models and systems that let me study these questions in practice.

I am Korean-Canadian and fluent in both English and Korean. Outside of work, you’ll usually find me on the jiu-jitsu mats.
